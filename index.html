🎓 Group 3 OS Presentation Guide: How to Demo Memory Management

Follow this simple step-by-step presentation script to demonstrate Segmentation and Demand Paging with True Page Faults & Evictions:

1

Explain the Architecture

Show the evaluators the Single-Page Two-Panel View: Left side is the Community Run desk (Registration, Runners, Bib & QR, Certificate). Right side is the OS Memory Management Unit (MMU).

🗣️ Say: "Our simulation combines segmentation and demand paging. Every runner lookup passes through the Segment Descriptor Table, checks bounds against the limit register, and queries the live Page Table."
2

Show Initial State (Pages on Secondary Storage)

Point to the Physical RAM Frames (empty) and Secondary Storage (Disk Swap Space): all registered runners reside on disk with Valid Bit = 0.

🗣️ Say: "In demand paging, pages are not brought into RAM until referenced. Initially, all runner records reside on disk swap."
3

Trigger the First Page Fault

Under QR Check-In Scanner, click the test button [⚡ Scan Bib 1001-6]. Watch the red PAGE FAULT flash! The MMU traps to INT 14, loads the page from disk into Frame #0, sets Valid Bit = 1, and displays the runner profile.

🗣️ Say: "Because Page #0 has Valid Bit = 0, a Page Fault occurred. The OS kernel allocated free Frame #0, loaded the record from secondary storage, and updated the Page Table."
4

Demonstrate an Instant Page Hit

Click [⚡ Scan Bib 1001-6] again. Watch the green PAGE HIT flash! Notice that the Hit counter increments, hit rate updates, and access occurs immediately from RAM without disk I/O.

🗣️ Say: "Now that Page #0 is resident in RAM with Valid Bit = 1, the MMU retrieves it directly with zero disk latency. The LRU timestamp and reference bit are updated."
5

Fill Frames & Trigger Eviction

Click bibs 1002-9, 2001-9, and 3001-2. All 4 RAM frames are now full! Now click bib 1003-2. Because RAM is full, the OS executes the Page Replacement Policy (FIFO or LRU), evicts the victim page back to disk, and places the new page into the freed frame!

🗣️ Say: "Under LRU, the least recently used frame is selected as the victim. Watch the amber eviction indicator and the live update in the Page Table and FIFO/LRU queue."
6

Demonstrate Dirty Bit (M = 1) & Heavy Load

Click ⏱️ Time on any runner to enter a finish time. Point to the Page Table: Dirty Bit M becomes 1! If that page is evicted later, the OS flushes changes to disk. Next, click ⚡ Heavy Load to show automated rapid lookups and frame churning.

7

Print Bib Card & Finisher Certificate

Click 🏷️ Bib to preview and print the official race bib card with QR code. Click 🏆 Cert to preview and print the official A4 Landscape Finisher Certificate.
